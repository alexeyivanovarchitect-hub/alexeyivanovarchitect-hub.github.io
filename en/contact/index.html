05 / Payment & Terms

Payment & Terms

Staged payments

Stage 1 — Initial Payment

50% payment before starting the work. Work begins after confirmation that the initial payment has been received.

Stage 2 — Milestone Payments

For projects consisting of several stages, payments are made according to the agreed project milestones.

Example: 50% — project start · 25% — intermediate milestone · 25% — final milestone.

The exact payment schedule can be agreed individually for each project before work begins.

Small Projects

For small projects or individual tasks, a 100% upfront payment may be used.

Large Projects

For larger projects, an individually agreed payment schedule is provided.

Payment & Terms

Work is carried out in stages according to the agreed scope of work and payment schedule. Each project stage may be associated with a separate payment. The project fee, scope of work, timeline, and payment schedule are agreed upon before the relevant stage begins.

Late Payment

If a payment is delayed, work on the project may be suspended until the relevant payment is received.
