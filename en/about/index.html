Ivanov Aleksei Vladimirovich

Architect

Aleksei Ivanov is an architect working across architectural design, urban planning, residential architecture, BIM, and architectural visualization.

Good afternoon, my name is Ivanov Aleksei Vladimirovich. I am an architect working in interior design, private residences, multi-storey residential buildings and public spaces.

My portfolio includes completed projects and concepts of different scales and types: private houses combining an individual architectural language with energy-efficiency requirements; multi-storey residential complexes within renovation programmes, aimed at modernising urban infrastructure; public spaces, including small architectural forms and landscaping to encourage social activity; and interior solutions for apartments, country residences and shared spaces, where the focus is on the interaction of functionality, aesthetics and ergonomics. I pay particular attention to detail and cross-disciplinary collaboration, supporting the visual integrity of projects and their long-term adaptability to changing conditions of use.

I am ready to collaborate on projects where architecture becomes a means of improving quality of life through innovative, conceptually grounded solutions.

I actively integrate tools from the modern BIM environment to optimise design, visualisation and project management throughout the project life cycle.

Approach

My professional philosophy is based on a people-centred approach, with the creation of comfortable, functional and emotionally engaging environments that meet the needs of contemporary society as its key priority.
