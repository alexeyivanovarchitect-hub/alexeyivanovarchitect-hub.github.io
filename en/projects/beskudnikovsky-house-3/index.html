ProjectResidential buildings under the renovation program

Beskudnikovsky Boulevard, House 3

Residential buildings under the renovation program in Moscow, at 3 Beskudnikovsky Boulevard. Public areas and retail premises are provided on the ground floors.

Architectural variety as a tool for humanizing urban space. The residential renovation program demonstrates how varied façade solutions can transform the urban landscape, bringing visual dynamism and a human scale. Replacing standard templates with individual design approaches—through textures, window rhythms or combinations of materials—creates an environment where aesthetics interact with functionality. This variety enlivens neighbourhoods and forms an inclusive atmosphere. Façades that reflect contemporary trends and local context become the “face” of a district, reducing visual monotony and strengthening residents’ connection to the place.
