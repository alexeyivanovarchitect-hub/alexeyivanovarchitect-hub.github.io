Open-Space Cell — Artemy Lebedev Studio

OPEN-SPACE cell concept for Artemy Lebedev Studio, at 7/1 Ulitsa 1905 Goda, Moscow. ICUBE architectural bureau.

“Creators”. Zoning open spaces while balancing functionality and aesthetics. Contemporary modular solutions for open space divide the environment into public and private areas and create a comfortable atmosphere for productive work. Lightweight timber-grid structures provide functional zoning and bring a sense of airiness to the interior. Their open texture softens the visual load while maintaining a connection to the shared space; natural materials add warmth to the minimalist aesthetic. These solutions show how tactile delicacy and geometric clarity can coexist in an environment where concentration and collaboration complement one another.

“Meeting Rooms”. Contemporary modular solutions for open space help transform former industrial spaces while retaining their historic character and adding functionality. In lofts and renovated workshops, open areas are divided into public and private zones while preserving the scale of industrial interiors. Lightweight structures soften the rough texture of industrial walls, and natural materials add warmth to the minimalist context. These solutions visually lighten large spaces and create a flexible environment that preserves the history of a place while supporting creativity and concentration.
