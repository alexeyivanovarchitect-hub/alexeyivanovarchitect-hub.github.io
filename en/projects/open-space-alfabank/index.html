Open-Space Cell — AlfaBank

Apple Meeting Rooms

Open-Space Cell Concept for ALFABANK, Moscow. Author: architect Aleksei Ivanov.

“Apple Meeting Rooms”. Contemporary modular solutions for open space divide the environment into public and private areas and create a comfortable atmosphere for productive work. Lightweight timber-lattice structures provide functional zoning and bring a sense of airiness to the interior. Their open texture softens the visual load while maintaining a connection to the shared space; natural materials add warmth to the minimalist aesthetic. These solutions demonstrate how tactile delicacy and geometric clarity can coexist in a harmonious environment.
