Architectural sketching

Type
Sketches
Places
India, Vietnam, Thailand, Laos
Technique
Hand drawing
Author
Aleksei Ivanov
Why draw

For me a sketch is a way of analysis, not just a record of what I saw. The line helps to read a city: the proportions of squares, the scale of facades, the neighbourhood of buildings from different eras.

What I look for

Street scenes, courtyards and markets show how people move through space and where public life is born. Most often it appears in the gaps between buildings.

What it gives to projects

Hand drawing keeps a sense of measure, the balance of function, beauty and memory of place. This experience then carries over into projects for clients.

IdeaMethod

The art of seeing and thinking

For me architectural sketching is not about capturing images but an analytical process. Drawing a city, you learn to read its layers, from historical context to the rhythm of new development, to understand the proportions of squares, the scale of facades and the dialogue between buildings of different eras.

A sketch teaches you to see not only objects but also the voids between them, where public life is born. Drawings of streets, courtyards and transport hubs show how people interact with space.

In the age of digital renderings, hand drawing preserves a sense of measure, the balance of function, aesthetics and urban memory that cannot be reduced to an algorithm.
