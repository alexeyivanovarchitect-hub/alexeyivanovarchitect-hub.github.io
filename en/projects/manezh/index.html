ProjectSports complex and public space

Manezh

A sports complex concept combined with retail for LEVEL GROUP. Located at Projected Passage No. 219, Moscow. It includes spaces for paddle tennis and street basketball, a café, public spaces and landscaping.

The concept integrates two key functions: contemporary sports infrastructure and a retail and café public space. The architectural solution is a single-storey volume with a pitched roof, integrated into the urban landscape. Panoramic glazing visually lightens the building, creating a sense of lightness and openness and connecting activity inside with the urban environment.
