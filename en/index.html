AboutRésumé

  

    
    

      
An artist’s eye, an architect’s experience

      
Instead of souvenirs, I bring back ideas for your home.

      
I was drawing before I became an architect. At Mosproekt, one of Moscow’s largest design institutes, and other firms I contributed to residential buildings that have since been built in Moscow, and a private house of my own design stands near Moscow. With colleagues I have designed interiors and developed concepts for sports complexes, mixed-use centres, residential districts, landscaping and public spaces.

      
Today I work remotely with clients from any country, designing apartments, houses, hotels and restaurants, and for larger projects I bring together a team. Drawing from life teaches me to see, a BIM model makes the drawings precise.
