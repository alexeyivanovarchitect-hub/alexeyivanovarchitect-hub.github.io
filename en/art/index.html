Sketches of Cultures

Original drawings from travels in Vietnam, Thailand, Laos, India and Bali. Every sheet was drawn from life and exists as a single original. Some works have already found their owners. To check whether a work you like is still available, send me its number on Telegram or WhatsApp. You buy directly from the artist.

The price depends on the sheet size and technique. Two works, 10% off. We will agree on delivery in the chat.

Route6 countries · 35 places · 46 stops

For 15 years I designed buildings in Moscow. Then I took a sketchbook and travelled across Asia: India, Thailand, Laos, Vietnam, Cambodia, Bali. 46 stops, almost all of it by bus.

I draw from life, right in the street: houses, temples, markets, boats, people. This is how I study a culture through line and colour.

Works in ink and fountain pen, watercolour, acrylic and oil. Sizes from A5 (148 × 210 mm) and A4 (210 × 297 mm) to sketchbook spreads of about 305 × 210 mm and canvases.

  • Hoi An, Vietnam

    A river port on the Thu Bon where ships from China, Japan and Europe called from the 15th to the 19th century. The old town keeps its wooden merchant houses, the Japanese Bridge and Chinese assembly halls, and has been a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 1999. A rare Asian trading town that has survived almost intact.

    Yellow walls remember
