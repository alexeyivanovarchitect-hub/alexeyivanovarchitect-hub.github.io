Architectural visualization and animation

Visualization and video are produced from the client’s source materials: a BIM or 3D model, drawings, renders, photographs. The brief records the purpose of the video, the fixed elements, style, storyboard, camera movement, duration and format.

Suitable for
For client presentations, marketing and property sales, competitions, portfolios and social media — the purpose is stated in the brief.
What is needed to start
A completed architectural animation brief and a link to the source files (formats and folder are listed in the brief).

01 / Scope of work

Scope of work

Source materials

  • Models: Archicad, Revit, 3ds Max, SketchUp, Rhino, Blender
  • BIM / 3D model, drawings, renders, photographs, existing video

What the brief defines

  • Purpose, audience and key message
  • Fixed elements: geometry, façades, floor plans, materials, surroundings, cameras
  • Style, lighting, materials, atmosphere and references
  • Storyboard, shot sequence and camera movement
  • People, vehicles, vegetation, water, weather
  • Sound, titles, logos, graphics

Deliverable format

  • Duration: from 10–15 seconds to 1–2 minutes, or as agreed
  • Aspect ratio: 16:9, 9:16, 1:1 or multiple formats
  • Resolution: Full HD, 4K or as agreed

No changes to the architecture or geometry are made without separate approval. The number of approval stages and the revision process are agreed before work begins.
