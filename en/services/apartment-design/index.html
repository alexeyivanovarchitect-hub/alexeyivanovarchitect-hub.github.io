Apartment design project

An apartment design project is a working set of drawings and visualizations used for renovation and fit-out. The composition of an actual set is shown below, using the design project for apartment No. 1461 as an example: from the demolition plan to visualizations and 360° panoramas.

Suitable for
For apartment owners who need a complete documentation set for renovation and for the contractor.
What is needed to start
A completed apartment brief. The required source information and project questions are listed in the brief itself.

01 / Scope of work

Scope of work

Plans

  • General data
  • Demolition plan
  • Plan of new partitions
  • Wall finishes layout
  • Shadow skirting layout
  • Floor plan
  • Underfloor heating plan
  • Ceiling plan
  • Furniture layout
  • Plumbing fixtures setting-out plan
  • Equipment layout
  • Sockets and electrical outlets setting-out plan
  • Lighting control groups plan

Wall elevations and sections

  • Wall elevations for each room
  • Sections with floor, wall and ceiling build-ups

Schedules

  • Room schedule
  • Schedule of new partitions
  • Floor preparation and finish schedules
  • Door schedule
  • Schedule of access hatches, window openings and shutters

Visualization

  • Room visualizations
  • 360° panoramas for each room (QR codes on the sheets)

The list follows the “General data” sheet of the design project for apartment No. 1461 (26 sheets). The set for your apartment is confirmed after the brief.

How collaboration begins

How collaboration begins

  1. Choose the relevant brief and complete it.
  2. Send the brief and source materials by email.
  3. After the brief is reviewed, the scope, timeline, fee and payment schedule are agreed.
  4. Work is carried out in stages according to the payment terms.

Prices and timelines are not published: they depend on the scope and are set after the brief has been reviewed.
